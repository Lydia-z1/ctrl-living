A COMPANY BUILT ON TRUST

Our principles

StudentRent HK is a student-housing decision-support prototype. Our business model is guided by these commitments:

  • Personal or individually identifiable user data will not be sold.
  • Commercial payments will not influence GIS suitability scores.
  • Sponsored placements will be clearly identified and kept separate from suitability scoring.
  • Proposed institutional reporting will use aggregated information rather than identifiable student records.
  • Core GIS functions will remain free. Additional paid features described in project materials are proposals, not services sold on this website.

This company site stores only your language preference in your browser. It does not collect enquiries or process payments. The linked ArcGIS app is a separate service.
